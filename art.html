Artist Directory

A curated selection of classic and contemporary artists whose visuals shaped and still shape rave culture.

Pez (Peter Thorpe)

One of the early pioneers in original rave art

Pez defined the look of early UK Flyers with hyper-colour characters, playful chaos and iconic rave artwork. His style became the visual language of the early rave explosion. Raindance, Helter Skelter, World of Dance... the list goes on!

Official site

Junior Tomlin

Airbrushed sci-fi futurism. A rave legend!

Often called the Salvador Dali of rave art, Junior Tomlin's airbrushed sci-fi landscapes, faces and chrome futurism gave Peace Fest, Dance Fest, Rave World and many other events their legendary cosmic identity.

Official site

Dan Pearce

Modern pop and rave art· Neon club energy

A leading contemporary artist whose work often channels club and rave energy. Collected by celebrities and represented by major galleries, his visuals bridge street culture and fine art.

Official site Clarendon Fine Art

Acer One

Graffiti infused art. Bristol legend!

Acer One graffiti driven style brought raw street energy to hardcore and jungle flyers, becoming a cult favourite among ravers and collectors of underground artwork.

Instagram

Goldie

Graffiti origins· Metalheadz iconography

Before becoming a jungle legend, Goldie was a graffiti artist whose visuals helped shape the early look of the scene. His Metalheadz iconography remains instantly recognisable worldwide.

Official site

Jonny Martin

Grassroots flyers and Underground visuals

Jonny Martin is known for authentic, underground rave artwork that captures the DIY spirit of the UK party scene, from flyers to posters and bespoke illustrations.

Instagram

Rebecca Try (RT)

The leading lady behind Kniteforce Records iconic artwork!

One of the few prominent female illustrators in the world of rave art, famous for the artwork associated with the infamous Kniteforce Records. RT's use of bright colours, awesome character and true hardcore street style make her one of the best artists in rave. Her compositions stand out in the visual history of rave culture.

Instagram

Dave Nodz

Suburban Base Records. A creator of true Hardcore/jungle identity

Dave Nodz was and continues to this day to be the primary visual architect behind Suburban Base Records, one of the most influential labels in early hardcore and jungle. His bold, comic style characters and graffiti infused layoutsdefined the label's iconic look and became a cornerstone of rave-art history.
